About Savanna Brew Café

Welcome to Savanna Brew Café, where the rich tradition of Kenyan coffee meets a warm and modern café experience. We are more than just a place to enjoy coffee; we are a space created for connection, relaxation and memorable moments.

Inspired by Kenya's beautiful landscapes, vibrant culture and locally grown coffee, our café brings together the comfort of a welcoming neighbourhood space and the experience of quality coffee and freshly prepared treats.

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café was created from an appreciation of Kenya's coffee culture and the simple pleasure of sharing good food and drinks with others.

We believe that a café should be somewhere people can take a break, meet friends, study, work or simply enjoy a quiet moment. This idea inspires the atmosphere and experience we aim to create every day.

From freshly brewed Kenyan coffee to delicious pastries and refreshing drinks, every part of our café is designed to make visitors feel welcome.

Meet Our Baristas

Behind every great cup of coffee is someone who understands the art of preparing it.

Our baristas bring skill, creativity and attention to detail to every cup. From preparing rich espresso to creating beautifully finished cappuccinos, they help make every visit a special experience.

We believe that good service begins with a friendly welcome and ends with a reason to come back.

Our Coffee and Food

Kenya is known for producing distinctive coffee with rich flavours and wonderful aromas. At Savanna Brew Café, we celebrate this coffee heritage through a selection of freshly prepared hot and cold beverages.

Our menu also includes pastries, light snacks and refreshing drinks, giving our customers something enjoyable to accompany every cup.

Our Mission

Our mission is to celebrate Kenyan coffee culture by offering quality beverages, delicious treats and a welcoming environment for everyone.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a memorable café destination where quality, community and Kenyan coffee culture come together.

What Makes Us Special?

We value locally inspired flavours, quality ingredients, friendly service and a comfortable atmosphere. Whether you are meeting friends, catching up on work or enjoying some time alone, we want every visit to feel like a little escape from the busy day.
